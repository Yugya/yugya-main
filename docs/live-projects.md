## 4. Screenshot method

- Headless Google Chrome, 1440 × 900 desktop and 390 × 844 mobile viewports.
- Light color scheme and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` for deterministic captures.
- Pages loaded from the live public URLs; no client site was modified.
- The desktop and mobile captures are separate genuine screenshots; the mobile capture is not a resized desktop image.
- Images were converted to WebP with Sharp and resized to the published dimensions.
- Desktop images: 1440 × 900. Mobile images: 390 × 844. Index thumbnails: 360 × 225.
- Every image has explicit width/height, lazy loading and descriptive alt text.
- The public cards label the captures "Desktop · Nov 2026" and "Mobile · Nov 2026".
