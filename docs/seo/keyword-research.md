# Keyword research — Nepal

Date: 2026-11-13
Market: Nepal (English, Romanized Nepali and Nepali-language queries)
Primary commercial objective: qualified website development enquiries.

## Method and honesty note

This research is based on:

- live SERP inspection for representative queries on 2026-11-13;
- inspection of the pages and result types that rank;
- the site's actual services and delivery model;
- Google's published guidance on intent, titles, helpful content and spam.

No paid keyword tool (Ahrefs, Semrush, Google Keyword Planner) was connected in this environment. **No search volume, keyword difficulty or traffic estimates are claimed.** Where a number appears in this document, it is either a source's published figure quoted as an observation or it is explicitly marked as unknown. Any future volume figures should be recorded with the tool, location, date and match type.

## 2. Primary keyword cluster

### Web development company in Nepal

- **Intent:** commercial. The searcher is comparing suppliers, not learning what a website is.
- **What ranks:** agency homepages with service lists, portfolios, client logos, pricing/quote CTAs; directory aggregators.
- **What the searcher needs:** proof of capability, location/remote model, process, contact path, evidence of real work.
- **Yugya's fit:** strong. The site has a real portfolio, documented process, founders, location and demos. The baseline weakness was the home title and the lack of an explicit "web development company in Nepal" statement in metadata.
- **Primary target page:** `/`
- **Supporting pages:** `/services/`, `/work/`, `/about/`, `/contact/`
- **Risk:** do not repeat the exact phrase unnaturally across the page. One clear title, one H1-adjacent statement and natural body copy are enough.

### Website design company in Nepal

- **Intent:** commercial, often earlier-stage than "web development".
- **What ranks:** design-led agency pages, portfolios, list articles.
- **Yugya's fit:** moderate. The site is engineering-led and deliberately restrained; it should not pretend to be a visual-design boutique.
- **Target:** `/services/#web-development` and `/work/`, not a separate page unless there is real design-specific content.

### Website developer in Nepal

- **Intent:** commercial, may include freelance intent.
- **Yugya's fit:** moderate. The studio positioning is a team, not a freelancer marketplace.
- **Target:** `/` and `/about/`, with the founders visible.

## 3. Cost cluster

### Website development cost in Nepal

- **Intent:** commercial investigation. Searchers are budgeting and comparing quotes.
- **What ranks:** long pricing guides with ranges such as NPR 20,000–50,000 for basic sites, 50,000–150,000 for business sites, and higher for e-commerce/custom apps (observed in competitor titles/snippets, not independently verified). Agency pages and blog posts.
- **Yugya's fit:** the studio does not publish fixed prices and should not invent them. The honest answer is a pricing-basis explanation: scope, pages/screens, integrations, content, server work, then a written quote.
- **Implemented:** one FAQ item on `/contact/` — "How is a project priced?" — with no invented numbers.
- **Future:** a full article, "What changes the cost of a website in Nepal", explaining cost drivers rather than publishing a fake rate card. It should link to `/services/` and `/contact/`.
- **Risk:** copying competitor price tables would be unoriginal and could be inaccurate for Yugya's scope.

## 4. Local cluster

### Butwal / Rupandehi / Bhairahawa

- **Intent:** local commercial. Searchers want someone nearby or at least in the same region.
- **What ranks:** local agencies with city pages, local directories, list articles. Examples observed: Inovex Media & Tech, Butwal Technologies, Infobytes Nepal, Sigma Nepal, Tech Temple, ABS Soft.
- **Yugya's actual position:** based in Kapilvastu / Rupandehi, Nepal; works with local organizations and remote clients. No street address, phone number or Google Business Profile is present in the repository.
- **Decision:** no city landing pages were created. Near-duplicate city pages with swapped names are explicitly covered by Google's doorway-abuse policy.
- **What to do instead:**
  1. Verify whether Yugya has a real, publishable business address and phone number.
  2. If eligible, create and verify a Google Business Profile with the real category, service area, hours, photos and contact details.
  3. Keep the location statement consistent on `/`, `/about/` and `/contact/`.
  4. Only create a Butwal/Rupandehi page if it contains genuinely local information: local project notes, local client work that the client permits, local service logistics, or a real office.
- **Target for now:** `/about/` (location section) and `/contact/`.

### Kathmandu / Pokhara / Chitwan / Biratnagar

- These are research candidates, not automatic page targets.
- Yugya can serve remote clients, but a city page with no local proof would be thin and risky.
- If the studio later has real clients or presence in a city, a single well-evidenced page is reasonable. Do not create a template across many cities.

## 5. Software and mobile cluster

### Software development company in Nepal / custom software development Nepal

- **Intent:** commercial, higher consideration than a brochure website.
- **What ranks:** agency service pages, directories, international firms.
- **Yugya's fit:** good for web applications and custom tooling, as demonstrated by the demos. The site does not claim native mobile app development.
- **Target:** `/services/#web-development` and `/work/` (CampusFlow, Stockwise, Worknest are business-tool examples).
- **Future content:** "Custom software vs ready-made software for a Nepali business" and "How to plan a custom software project".

### Mobile app development in Nepal

- **Intent:** commercial.
- **Yugya's fit:** unclear from the current site. The mission brief lists mobile app development as a secondary service, but no page, project or service scope currently supports it.
- **Decision:** do not add mobile app claims to metadata or schema until the business confirms the service and there is real content/proof. This avoids an unsupported claim.
- **Next step:** if confirmed, create a proper mobile app service section or page with stack, deliverables and real examples.

### AI integration services Nepal / AI automation services Nepal

- **Intent:** commercial, growing but noisy.
- **What ranks:** dedicated AI agencies and consultancies.
- **Yugya's fit:** partial. The studio offers business automation and has its own AI product (Yugs), but the site does not sell "AI integration" as a standalone service.
- **Decision:** do not rebrand the whole site as an AI agency. The existing automation service and Yugs section are accurate.
- **Future content:** "Practical AI automation for Nepali businesses" focused on document/data workflows and human review, linking to `/services/#automation` and Yugs.

## 6. Domain and hosting cluster

### .com.np domain registration

- **Intent:** informational with transactional follow-through.
- **What ranks:** register.com.np (the national registry) and guide articles.
- **Yugya's fit:** the `/services/#domain-dns` section explicitly covers `.np` paperwork, cover letters, DNS, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and DNSSEC.
- **Target:** `/services/#domain-dns`.
- **Future content:** a genuinely useful `.com.np` guide that cites register.com.np for eligibility, documents and process, and explains common rejection reasons. It must be based on the actual current registry requirements, not copied from another agency.
- **Risk:** facts change; verify with the registry before publishing.

### Web hosting Nepal / VPS hosting Nepal / server management Nepal

- **Intent:** commercial.
- **What ranks:** hosting companies, agency pages, guides.
- **Yugya's fit:** Linux VPS setup, hardening, deployment and maintenance. The site explicitly does not resell shared hosting.
- **Target:** `/services/#server-infrastructure` and `/services/#maintenance`.
- **Future content:** "VPS vs shared hosting for a Nepali business" and "What a documented server handover includes".

### Website maintenance Nepal

- **Intent:** commercial.
- **Yugya's fit:** strong. Monthly patching, TLS checks, backups, link fixes and direct engineer contact.
- **Target:** `/services/#maintenance`.
- **Future content:** a maintenance checklist article.
